    GTFS service

    The GTFS module provides a semantic representation of public transport services based on the General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) standard. It describes the structure and operation of the transport network through transport agencies, routes, trips, stops, service calendars, schedules, fares and transfer rules. It also incorporates concepts related to flexible transport services, including booking rules and pickup and drop-off areas.

    By representing GTFS data in OWL and integrating GeoSPARQL, the module establishes explicit relationships between transport services and their geographical environment.

    Within the Urban-Mobility-Ontology, this module represents the public transport supply and connects it to passenger demand. It supports the identification of potential connections between scheduled and on-demand transport services, the exploration of alternative travel options and the integration of DRT services into the existing public transport network.
