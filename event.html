    Events and impacts

    The Events module represents disruptive events that may affect the operation of urban transport services, such as road accidents, roadworks, vehicle breakdowns and road closures. Its objective is to describe the nature of these events, their spatial and temporal extent, and their consequences for the transport system.

    The module distinguishes between the occurrence of an event and its resulting impacts. An event is characterized by its type, geographical location, starting time and ending time, while its impacts may concern road infrastructure, public transport stops, transport actors or passenger travel plans.

    The representation relies on the reuse of the Simple Event Model (SEM), the Transport Disruption Ontology (Dtx), the Event Ontology and OWL-Time.

    By connecting disruptive events with the Infrastructure, GTFS and DRT modules, this module supports the identification of affected road segments, transport stops and passenger reservations, providing a semantic foundation for disruption-aware transport planning and decision-making.
