    DRT core

    The Demand-Responsive Transport (DRT) module focuses on the representation of passenger mobility demand and its relationship with the organization of on-demand transport services operated by autonomous shuttles.

    The module describes each transport request through its spatial, temporal and individual constraints, including pickup and drop-off locations, departure and arrival time windows, booking notice, maximum acceptable travel duration and accessibility requirements.

    The modeling distinguishes between the customer who makes a reservation, the passengers associated with it, the individual trips and the vehicle rides assigned to serve the requested journeys. This distinction makes it possible to represent shared rides while preserving the specific mobility requirements of each reservation.

    As the central component of the Urban-Mobility-Ontology, the DRT module integrates passenger demand with the public transport supply (GTFS), the urban environment (Infrastructure) and disruptive events (Events). This integrated representation provides a basis for analyzing passenger mobility needs, identifying alternative public transport services and assessing the impact of disruptions on planned journeys.
